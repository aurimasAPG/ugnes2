# Hidden Valley art style (generated assets)

- Tone: wet stone, industrial-agricultural, slightly haunted; not whimsical or cute.
- Palette: cool greys, moss green, damp bark brown, pale channel sand, cold water blue-grey.
- Surfaces: seamless tileable albedo textures, soft diffuse, no hard shadows, no logos.
- Characters: stylized 3D-game readable silhouettes on flat keyable backgrounds; Pip is a lamp-moth (not fairy).
- Icons: square inventory icons, same stroke/fill weight, muted metal/glass/organic materials, no text.
