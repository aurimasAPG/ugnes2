## What this file needs to contain before Phase 3

Listed so that supplying it is a checklist rather than a paste-and-hope. Only the
material the vertical slice actually consumes is listed — the other six regions are
out of scope and their sections can stay wherever they currently live.

- **Heartwood Village** — what it is, who lives there, why it is where it is, what
  the player is meant to feel walking in.
- **The forest region** — the one adjacent region in scope. Its relationship to
  Heartwood, and what makes it legible as a different place.
- **Pip** — the companion. What Pip is, how Pip talks, and what Pip is *for*
  mechanically, not just tonally.
- **The three NPCs** — names and character material. Note that the mission
  constrains them at the systems level (one grants a crafting recipe family, one
  changes what the world contains, one gates traversal); the bible supplies who
  they are, not what they unlock.
- **The mystery thread** — the full thread, including its resolution and the
  unanswered question it lands on. This is the one piece that cannot be authored
  incrementally, because the hook test in kill criterion 3 measures exactly whether
  its information design works.
- **Naming conventions** — how things in this world are named, so that new names
  are derivable rather than invented per-asset.

## Note on the derivativeness pass

Whatever arrives here goes through §3.3 before it reaches content. Names and
silhouettes from the original document are not exempt — the pass asks what specific
existing game each one came from, and the answer being "our own earlier draft" is
not an answer.
